> Last updated: 2026-03-11
> Source: LA County Department of Regional Planning

## Program Overview

LA County offers **three pre-approved ADU plans** for properties in unincorporated LA County. These plans have been pre-reviewed and approved by the Department of Regional Planning, reducing the permit review process for homeowners in unincorporated areas.

## Review Timeline Benefits

| Application Type | Typical Review Period |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Pre-approved plan** | 2-4 weeks to permit |
| Custom plan | 6-12 weeks to permit |
| **Time savings** | 4-8 weeks faster |

Using a pre-approved plan provides several timeline advantages:

- **Reduced plan check** — structural, architectural, and code compliance already verified
- **Fewer corrections** — pre-approved plans have been vetted for common issues
- **Faster building permit issuance** — county staff only review site-specific conditions
- **Predictable process** — contractors and inspectors are familiar with the standard designs

---

## Site-Specific Requirements

Even with a pre-approved plan, the county will review:

- **Zoning compliance** — setbacks (4 ft min side/rear), lot coverage, FAR
- **Foundation/soils** — geotechnical report may be required based on site conditions
- **Utilities** — sewer/septic capacity, water connection, electrical service
- **Fire/access** — especially in Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones (common in hillside unincorporated areas)
- **Grading** — hillside lots may require grading permits

---

## Eligible Areas

Pre-approved plans are available for properties in **unincorporated LA County** only. This includes communities such as:

- Altadena
- East Los Angeles
- Florence-Firestone
- Hacienda Heights
- La Crescenta-Montrose
- Lennox
- Rowland Heights
- South Whittier
- Topanga
- Walnut Park
- West Athens
- Willowbrook

Properties within incorporated cities (e.g., City of LA, Pasadena, Glendale) must follow their respective city's ADU programs.

---

## How to Apply

1. **Verify** your property is in unincorporated LA County (use [planning.lacounty.gov](https://planning.lacounty.gov) parcel lookup)
2. **Select** Plan A, B, or C based on your lot size and needs
3. **Prepare** site-specific documents: plot plan showing ADU placement, setbacks, existing structures
4. **Submit** permit application to LA County Department of Regional Planning
5. **County reviews** site-specific conditions (zoning, utilities, fire, grading)
6. **Building permit issued** upon approval
7. **Construction** and inspections per standard building permit process
