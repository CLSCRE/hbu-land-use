## Program Overview

The City of Los Angeles Standard Plan Program offers **87+ pre-approved ADU designs** created by licensed architects and engineers. These plans have been pre-reviewed by LADBS for compliance with building, residential, and green codes, significantly reducing permit review time.

**Key benefit:** Using a standard plan shortens plan check review and permits are issued more quickly than custom-designed ADUs.

### How It Works

1. Browse the catalog of approved standard plans
2. Select a plan that fits your property and needs
3. Submit permit application referencing the standard plan number
4. LADBS reviews for property-specific fit: zoning compliance, lot coverage, setbacks, and foundation requirements
5. Permit issued faster than custom plan review (days vs. weeks)

## How to Apply

1. **Visit:** [dbs.lacity.gov/adu](https://dbs.lacity.gov/adu)
2. **Select** a standard plan from the approved catalog
3. **Prepare** site-specific documents: plot plan, soils report (if required), Title 24 energy compliance
4. **Submit** permit application online or at LADBS office
5. **LADBS reviews** for property fit — zoning, setbacks, lot coverage, foundation
6. **Permit issued** upon approval

---

## Key Advantages of Standard Plans

- **Faster approval** — pre-reviewed for code compliance
- **Lower design costs** — no need to hire architect from scratch
- **Proven designs** — already built and tested across LA
- **Reduced risk** — fewer plan check corrections and resubmittals
- **Predictable costs** — contractors familiar with standard plan construction
