# California ADU Legislation — Comprehensive Summary

> Last updated: 2026-03-11
> Source: California Legislative Information (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov)

---

## AB 68 (2019) — ADUs By-Right in All Residential Zones

- **Effective:** January 1, 2020 (approved October 9, 2019)
- **Author:** Ting
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Requires ministerial (by-right) approval of one ADU and one JADU per single-family lot
  - Allows detached ADUs with 4-foot side and rear setbacks, max 800 SF, 16 ft height on any residential lot
  - Permits multiple ADUs within existing multifamily structures using non-livable space
  - Up to two detached ADUs allowed on multifamily lots
  - Eliminates minimum lot size requirements for ADUs
  - Reduces permit processing timeline to 60 days for completed applications on lots with existing dwellings
- **Citation:** [AB 68 (2019-2020)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=201920200AB68)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.2

## SB 13 (2019) — Impact Fee Exemptions for ADUs < 750 SF

- **Effective:** January 1, 2020 (approved October 9, 2019)
- **Author:** Wieckowski
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Prohibits local agencies from imposing impact fees on ADUs under 750 square feet
  - Impact fees for ADUs 750 SF or larger must be charged proportionally relative to the primary dwelling's square footage
  - Eliminates utility connection fees for ADUs within existing structures
  - Connection charges for other ADUs must be proportionate to burden (based on SF or drainage fixture units)
  - No replacement parking required when demolishing garages/carports for ADU construction
  - Parking exemptions for ADUs within 1/2 mile of public transit, in historic districts, or within primary residence
  - 60-day approval timeline; automatic approval if agency fails to act
  - Minimum maximum sizes: 850 SF (studio/1BR) or 1,000 SF (2+ BR)
- **Citation:** [SB 13 (2019-2020)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=201920200SB13)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.2

## AB 881 (2019) — Prohibits Owner-Occupancy Requirements (Temporary)

- **Effective:** January 1, 2020 (approved October 9, 2019)
- **Author:** Bloom
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Prohibited local agencies from imposing owner-occupancy requirements on ADUs through January 1, 2025 (sunset clause)
  - Reduced required setbacks from 5 feet to 4 feet for side and rear lot lines on newly constructed ADUs
  - Eliminated parking replacement requirements when garages are demolished or converted for ADU use
  - 60-day permit processing timeline for completed applications
  - Prohibited minimum SF requirements that would block efficiency units
  - Established minimum maximum sizes: 850 SF (studio/1BR), 1,000 SF (2+ BR)
  - Eliminated minimum lot size requirements
- **Citation:** [AB 881 (2019-2020)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=201920200AB881)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.2

## AB 2221 (2022) — Extends Owner-Occupancy Ban Through 2025

- **Effective:** January 1, 2023 (approved September 28, 2022)
- **Author:** Quirk-Silva
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Explicitly prohibits local agencies from imposing owner-occupancy requirements on ADUs before January 1, 2025
  - Retroactive protection: ADUs permitted between January 1, 2020 and January 1, 2025 are exempt from owner-occupancy mandates even if imposed later
  - After January 1, 2025, agencies may optionally require owner-occupancy for either the primary dwelling or the ADU on single-family lots
  - Agencies may require ADU rentals be for terms longer than 30 days (short-term rental prohibition)
- **Citation:** [AB 2221 (2021-2022)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202120220AB2221)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.2

## SB 897 (2022) — Height Increases for ADUs

- **Effective:** January 1, 2023 (approved September 28, 2022)
- **Author:** Wieckowski
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Height limits by type:**
    - Standard detached ADU: 16 feet
    - Detached ADU within 1/2 mile of major transit stop or high-quality transit corridor: **18 feet**
    - Detached ADU on multifamily lot with existing multistory dwelling: **18 feet**
    - Attached ADU: **25 feet** (or primary dwelling zoning limit, whichever is lower), 2-story maximum
  - Local agencies must apply only objective standards to ADU approvals (no subjective judgment)
  - Cannot deny ADU permits based on existing zoning violations or code issues not threatening public safety
  - Garage demolition permits must be processed simultaneously with ADU construction permits
  - Enhanced parking exemptions for ADUs paired with new construction
  - Clarifies attached garages count as part of primary residence for JADU purposes
- **Citation:** [SB 897 (2021-2022)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202120220SB897)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.2

## AB 916 (2022) — Streamlined Bedroom Additions (Related to JADU Conversions)

- **Effective:** January 1, 2023 (approved September 28, 2022)
- **Author:** Salas
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Prohibits local agencies from requiring public hearings when property owners reconfigure existing interior space to add bedrooms
  - Applies to applications for up to two additional bedrooms within an existing dwelling unit
  - Does not apply to projects increasing the number of dwelling units within a structure
  - Declares adequate housing a matter of statewide concern, applying to all cities including charter cities
  - Facilitates JADU conversions by reducing procedural barriers to interior reconfigurations
- **Citation:** [AB 916 (2021-2022)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202120220AB916)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.23

## AB 1033 (2023) — Separate Sale of ADUs as Condominiums

- **Effective:** January 1, 2024 (approved October 11, 2023)
- **Author:** Ting
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Authorizes local agencies to adopt ordinances permitting separate conveyance (sale) of primary dwelling and ADU as condominiums
  - ADUs must be created under the Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act
  - Must follow all objective requirements of the Subdivision Map Act and local subdivision ordinances
  - Safety inspection required before recording condominium plan (certificate of occupancy or HUD-certified inspection)
  - Lienholder consent required before recording — lienholders can refuse or impose conditions
  - Extensive consumer disclosure requirements (refinancing, lender approval, modification consent)
  - Utility providers (water, sewer, gas, electric) must be notified
  - HOA written authorization required for properties in existing planned developments
- **Citation:** [AB 1033 (2023-2024)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240AB1033)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.26

## SB 477 (2023) — Code Reorganization and Renumbering

- **Effective:** March 25, 2024
- **Author:** Wieckowski
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Reorganizes and renumbers Government Code sections relevant to state ADU and JADU law
  - Makes nonsubstantive changes to consolidate various ADU provisions
  - Requires local governments to develop plans incentivizing ADUs offered at affordable rent for lower-income households
  - Existing ADU approval processes, timelines, and standards remain governed by reorganized code sections
  - Moved ADU provisions to Article 2 (commencing with Section 66314) and Article 3 (commencing with Section 66333) of Chapter 13
- **Citation:** [SB 477 (2023-2024)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240SB477)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Chapter 13

---

## AB 976 (2024) — Makes ADU Streamlining Permanent (Removes Sunset)

- **Effective:** January 1, 2025 (approved October 11, 2023)
- **Author:** Ting
- **Key Provisions:**
  - **Permanently eliminates owner-occupancy requirements** for ADUs — removes the January 1, 2025 sunset clause from AB 881/AB 2221
  - No local agency may impose owner-occupancy requirements on ADUs going forward
  - Adjusts rental term provisions: agencies may require rentals of 30 days or longer (previously required "longer than 30 days")
  - Adds condominium conversion authority for local agencies (consistent with AB 1033)
  - Makes all ADU permit streamlining provisions permanent — no future sunset dates
- **Citation:** [AB 976 (2023-2024)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240AB976)
- **Code Section:** Government Code Section 65852.2 / renumbered sections

## Key State Minimums (Current Law)

| Standard | Minimum Allowed |
|----------|----------------|
| Max ADU Size (local cannot go below) | 850 SF (studio/1BR), 1,000 SF (2+ BR) |
| Max Height — Detached | 16 ft standard, 18 ft near transit |
| Max Height — Attached | 25 ft or zoning limit (2-story max) |
| Setbacks — Side & Rear | 4 ft maximum |
| Permit Processing | 60 days for completed application |
| Impact Fees — Under 750 SF | Prohibited |
| Owner-Occupancy | Cannot be required (permanent as of AB 976) |
| JADU Size | Up to 500 SF within primary residence |
