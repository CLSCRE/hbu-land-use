# LA County ADU Ordinance -- Title 22, Section 22.140.640

**Jurisdiction:** Unincorporated Los Angeles County
**Code Section:** Title 22 -- Planning and Zoning, Division 6, Chapter 22.140 (General Provisions), Part 23 -- Accessory Dwelling Units and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units, Section 22.140.640
**Administering Agency:** LA County Department of Regional Planning (DRP) and Department of Public Works -- Building and Safety
**Latest Amendment Effective Date:** August 28, 2025 (Board of Supervisors approved October 2024)
**Applicable State Law:** California Government Code Section 65852.2 (ADU) and Section 65852.22 (JADU)

---

## 1. Overview

Section 22.140.640 of the Los Angeles County Code establishes development standards and case processing procedures for Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units (JADUs) in the **unincorporated areas** of Los Angeles County. The ordinance implements California's statewide ADU laws while incorporating local development standards where permitted by state law.

An **ADU** is defined as a dwelling unit with complete independent living facilities (kitchen, bathroom, sleeping area) that is attached to, located within, or detached from a single-family or multi-family residential building.

A **JADU** is defined as a dwelling unit no larger than 500 square feet, with a separate exterior entrance, located entirely within an existing or proposed single-family residence, including within an attached garage.

## 2. Key Provisions

### 2.1 Permitted Number of ADUs

| Lot Type | ADUs Allowed | JADUs Allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Single-family residential (SFR) lot | 1 ADU | 1 JADU |
| Multi-family residential (MFR) lot | 2 new detached ADUs + at least 1 conversion ADU (from non-habitable space) | Not permitted |

### 2.2 Maximum Unit Size

| ADU Type | Maximum Floor Area |
|---|---|
| Detached ADU | 1,200 sq. ft. |
| Attached ADU | 1,200 sq. ft. or 50% of existing residence, whichever is less |
| JADU | 500 sq. ft. |
| State-mandated minimum (by right) | 800 sq. ft. for a detached ADU on an SFR lot |

**Note:** California Government Code Section 65852.2(c) guarantees that a local agency must allow at least an 800-square-foot detached ADU on any lot with an existing or proposed single-family dwelling, regardless of local floor area ratio (FAR), lot coverage, or open space requirements.

### 2.3 Height Limits

| Scenario | Maximum Height |
|---|---|
| Detached ADU (standard) | 16 feet |
| Detached ADU within 1/2 mile of a major transit stop or high-quality transit corridor | 18 feet |
| Detached ADU on a lot with an existing or proposed multi-story, multi-family dwelling | 18 feet |
| ADU above an existing or new detached garage | 20 feet (per SB 897 provisions) |
| Attached ADU | Must not exceed the height of the primary structure or 25 feet, whichever is less |

### 2.4 Setback Requirements

| Setback Type | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Side yard (new construction ADU) | 4 feet minimum |
| Rear yard (new construction ADU) | 4 feet minimum |
| Front yard | Per underlying zone (Title 22 base zone standards) |
| Conversion of existing structure | No setbacks required if built in the same location and dimensions |
| Attached ADU (to primary) | No separation required from primary structure |

### 2.5 Parking Requirements

**General Rule:** One uncovered parking space is required per ADU (minimum dimensions: 8.5 ft. x 18 ft.).

**Parking Exemptions -- No parking required when:**
- The ADU is located within 1/2 mile of a public transit stop
- The ADU is located within an architecturally and historically significant historic district
- The ADU is part of an existing primary residence or accessory structure (conversion)
- The ADU is located outside a Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone (VHFHSZ)
- On-street parking permits are required but not offered to the ADU occupant
- A car-share vehicle is located within one block of the ADU

**Garage Conversions:** No replacement parking is required when a garage, carport, or covered parking structure is converted to an ADU or demolished in conjunction with ADU construction.

**JADU Parking:** No parking is required for JADUs.

### 2.6 JADU-Specific Rules

- Maximum size: 500 sq. ft.
- Must be contained entirely within the walls of an existing or proposed single-family residence (including attached garage)
- Must have a separate exterior entrance
- May include a shared bathroom with the primary residence (an efficiency kitchen is permitted instead of a full kitchen)
- **Owner-occupancy requirement:** Either the JADU or the primary SFR must be owner-occupied (note: per AB 976, effective January 1, 2025, owner-occupancy requirements for standard ADUs are eliminated statewide, but JADU owner-occupancy remains)
- Cannot be sold separately from the primary residence

### 2.7 Additional Standards

- **Short-Term Rental:** ADUs may only be used as short-term rentals (30 consecutive days or fewer) if the owner's primary residence is the ADU itself.
- **Separate Sale Prohibition:** An ADU or JADU cannot be sold separately from the primary dwelling, except as provided under Government Code Section 65852.26 (separate conveyance by qualified nonprofits or affordable housing organizations).
- **Low Impact Development (LID):** ADUs of 500 sq. ft. or more in unincorporated LA County must comply with LID requirements for stormwater management.
- **Impact Fees:** ADUs under 750 sq. ft. are exempt from impact fees. ADUs 750 sq. ft. or larger are subject to impact fees proportional to the primary dwelling's fee obligation.
- **Utility Connections:** A new or separate utility connection may be required for a detached ADU; connection fees must be proportionate to the ADU's burden on the system.
- **Deed Restriction:** A deed restriction is recorded with the County Recorder acknowledging ADU/JADU regulations, including occupancy, sale, and size limitations.
- **Coastal Zone:** In Coastal Zones, ADUs and JADUs are subject to the applicable Local Coastal Program rather than Section 22.140.640.

## 3. How LA County Differs from California State Law

California state law (Government Code Sections 65852.2 and 65852.22) establishes minimum standards that all local jurisdictions must meet. Local agencies may adopt ordinances that are **equal to or more permissive** than state law but may not be more restrictive in key areas. The table below compares LA County's ordinance to the state baseline.

| Standard | State Law Minimum | LA County (Unincorporated) |
|---|---|---|
| Ministerial approval timeline | 60 days | Compliant -- 60-day ministerial review |
| Max detached ADU size | At least 800 sq. ft. by right | 1,200 sq. ft. (more permissive) |
| Max attached ADU size | At least 800 sq. ft. or 50% of primary | 1,200 sq. ft. or 50% of primary, whichever is less (aligned) |
| Height -- standard detached | 16 ft. minimum | 16 ft. (aligned) |
| Height -- near transit | 18 ft. minimum | 18 ft. (aligned) |
| Height -- above garage | 20 ft. (per SB 897) | 20 ft. (aligned) |
| Side/rear setbacks | 4 ft. maximum that localities can impose | 4 ft. (aligned at state maximum) |
| Parking | Can require max 1 space; many exemptions | 1 space with exemptions (aligned) |
| Owner occupancy (ADU) | Cannot require (per AB 976, eff. 1/1/2025) | Compliant -- no owner-occupancy for ADUs |
| Owner occupancy (JADU) | Required (either JADU or SFR) | Required (aligned) |
| Garage conversion parking replacement | Cannot require | Not required (aligned) |
| Short-term rental restrictions | Allowed per local ordinance | Restricted -- only if owner lives in ADU |
| Fire hazard zone (VHFHSZ) parking | State allows parking requirement in VHFHSZ | County requires parking in VHFHSZ (aligned) |
| LID / stormwater requirements | Not addressed in state ADU law | Required for ADUs >= 500 sq. ft. (local addition) |

**Key local additions beyond state law:**
1. **Low Impact Development (LID)** requirements for ADUs 500 sq. ft. or larger
2. **Short-term rental restrictions** limiting vacation rental use
3. **Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone** parking requirements retained where state law permits

## 4. Unincorporated Areas vs. Incorporated Cities

This is a critical distinction for LA County. The County contains **88 incorporated cities** (e.g., City of Los Angeles, Pasadena, Long Beach, Santa Monica) and large swaths of **unincorporated territory** (e.g., East Los Angeles, Altadena, Topanga, Hacienda Heights, Rowland Heights, La Crescenta-Montrose, Willowbrook).

| Factor | Unincorporated LA County | Incorporated Cities |
|---|---|---|
| Governing ordinance | Title 22, Section 22.140.640 | Each city adopts its own ADU ordinance |
| Permitting authority | LA County DRP + Public Works Building & Safety | City planning and building departments |
| Development standards | County code (described in this document) | Vary by city; must meet state minimums |
| Height limits | 16-20 ft. (see table above) | Vary; City of LA allows up to 25 ft. for attached ADUs |
| Max ADU size | 1,200 sq. ft. | Varies; some cities cap at 800-1,000 sq. ft. |
| Short-term rental | Restricted | Varies by city ordinance |
| Pre-approved plans | County program (see Section 5) | Some cities have their own programs (e.g., City of LA's YOU-ADU) |
| Impact fees | Exempt under 750 sq. ft. | Varies by city |

**Important:** If a property is located within an **incorporated city** in LA County, the city's ADU ordinance applies -- not the County's. The County's ordinance under Title 22 applies **only** to unincorporated areas. Homeowners should verify whether their property is in an incorporated or unincorporated area using the [LA County GIS Portal](https://lacounty.gov/gis/) or by contacting DRP.

## 5. Pre-Approved ADU Standard Plan Program

LA County operates a **Pre-Approved ADU Standard Plan Program** through the Department of Public Works -- Building and Safety Division, designed to expedite the review and approval process for homeowners building ADUs in unincorporated areas.

### Available Plans (County-Owned -- Free)

| Plan | Size | Configuration |
|---|---|---|
| Plan A | 1,200 sq. ft. | 3 bedrooms, 1 bathroom |
| Plan B | 1,200 sq. ft. | 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom |
| Plan C | 800 sq. ft. | 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom |

### Program Details

- **Cost:** County-owned plans are available **free of charge** to homeowners
- **Modifications:** Plans cannot be modified and must be permitted as designed; however, they may be oriented in any direction or mirrored (flipped)
- **Site Requirements:** A site-specific plot plan approved by Regional Planning is required to match the plan to the specific parcel
- **Energy Compliance:** Site-specific Title 24 energy calculations are required
- **Submission:** Applications are submitted through **EPIC-LA**, the County's online permitting portal
- **Third-Party Plans:** Additional pre-approved plans from private design firms will be made available for purchase directly from those firms (ongoing program expansion)

### Program Benefits

- Faster plan check and permit issuance (reduced review scope since structural and architectural plans are pre-reviewed)
- Lower design costs for homeowners who do not need custom plans
- Code-compliant designs that meet all current County building standards

**Program URL:** [https://pw.lacounty.gov/building-and-safety/adu/pre-approved](https://pw.lacounty.gov/building-and-safety/adu/pre-approved)

## 6. Permit Process Summary

1. **Determine Eligibility:** Verify the property is in unincorporated LA County and zoned for residential use
2. **Site Plan Review:** Submit a site plan to DRP for zoning clearance and plot plan approval
3. **Plan Submission:** Submit construction plans (custom or pre-approved) to Public Works Building & Safety via EPIC-LA
4. **Agency Review:** Plans are reviewed for compliance with Title 22 zoning, California Building Code, fire safety, and environmental requirements
5. **Permit Issuance:** Building permit issued upon approval (ministerial process; no discretionary review or public hearing required)
6. **Construction & Inspection:** Build per approved plans; schedule required inspections
7. **Final Approval:** Certificate of occupancy or final sign-off issued after final inspection
8. **Deed Restriction:** Record a deed restriction with the LA County Recorder acknowledging ADU/JADU restrictions

**Processing Time:** State law mandates a maximum 60-day review period for ministerially approved ADU applications.
