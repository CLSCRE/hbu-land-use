## Detailed Rules by Jurisdiction

### City of Los Angeles

- **Ordinance:** LAMC Section 12.22 A.31
- **Contact:** LA Department of Building and Safety (DBS) — [dbs.lacity.gov/adu](https://dbs.lacity.gov/adu)
- **Max ADU Size:**
  - Detached: 1,200 SF
  - Attached: 50% of existing living area, up to 1,200 SF
  - Conversion of existing space: no size limit
- **Max Height:**
  - Detached: 16 ft (standard), 18 ft (within 1/2 mile of major transit)
  - Attached: 25 ft or existing zoning height limit, whichever is lower
- **Setbacks:** 4 ft side and rear for new construction; no setback for conversions within existing footprint
- **JADU:** Yes, up to 500 SF, must be within existing primary residence or attached garage
- **Parking:** No parking required for ADUs or JADUs (citywide policy)
- **Owner-Occupancy:** Not required (prohibited by state law per AB 976)
- **Pre-Approved Plans:** Yes — 87+ Standard Plan Program designs available through LADBS
- **Permit Timeline:** 60 days (standard plans faster)
- **Notes:** Most permissive local ADU rules in the county. Standard Plan Program significantly accelerates approvals.

### Unincorporated LA County

- **Ordinance:** Title 22 (Zoning Code), Chapter 22.140
- **Contact:** LA County Department of Regional Planning — [planning.lacounty.gov/adu](https://planning.lacounty.gov/adu)
- **Max ADU Size:**
  - Detached: 1,200 SF
  - Attached: 50% of existing living area, up to 1,200 SF
  - Conversion of existing space: no size limit beyond state minimums
- **Max Height:**
  - Detached: 16 ft (standard), 18 ft (near transit or on multifamily lots)
  - Attached: 25 ft or zoning limit
- **Setbacks:** 4 ft side and rear
- **JADU:** Yes, up to 500 SF within primary residence
- **Parking:** Generally not required; exemptions per state law
- **Owner-Occupancy:** Not required
- **Pre-Approved Plans:** Yes — 3 pre-approved plans (Studio 400 SF, 1BR 600 SF, 2BR 1,200 SF)
- **Permit Timeline:** 60 days
- **Notes:** Covers large unincorporated areas including East LA, Altadena, Hacienda Heights, Rowland Heights, etc.

### Santa Monica

- **Ordinance:** SMMC Chapter 9.31.025
- **Contact:** Planning & Community Development — [smgov.net](https://www.smgov.net/Departments/PCD/)
- **Max ADU Size:**
  - Detached: 900 SF
  - Attached: 850 SF
  - State minimum override: 850 SF (1BR) or 1,000 SF (2+ BR) regardless of local cap
- **Max Height:**
  - Detached: 16 ft (standard), 18 ft (near transit — much of city qualifies)
  - Attached: 25 ft / 2-story max
- **Setbacks:** 4 ft side and rear
- **JADU:** Yes, up to 500 SF within primary residence
- **Parking:** Not required within 1/2 mile of transit (covers most of city); otherwise 1 space
- **Owner-Occupancy:** Not required
- **Pre-Approved Plans:** No formal program
- **Permit Timeline:** 60 days
- **Notes:** Smaller lot sizes make detached ADUs more challenging. High land values make ADU ROI attractive.

### Pasadena

- **Ordinance:** PMC Title 17.50.280
- **Contact:** Planning & Community Development — [cityofpasadena.net/planning](https://www.cityofpasadena.net/planning/accessory-dwelling-units/)
- **Max ADU Size:**
  - Detached: 1,200 SF
  - Attached: 50% of existing or 1,200 SF
- **Max Height:**
  - Detached: 16 ft (standard), 18 ft (near transit — Gold Line stations qualify)
  - Attached: 25 ft / 2-story
- **Setbacks:** 4 ft side and rear
- **JADU:** Yes, up to 500 SF within primary residence or attached garage
- **Parking:** 1 space per unit or per bedroom (whichever is less); exemptions apply if within 1/2 mile of transit, in historic district, within existing structure, or near car-share
- **Owner-Occupancy:** Not required (state law override)
- **Pre-Approved Plans:** No formal program
- **Permit Timeline:** 60 days
- **Notes:** Historic district overlay may add design review requirements for visibility from public right-of-way. ADUs not visible from street have fewer restrictions.

## State Law Minimums (All Jurisdictions Must Allow)

Per Government Code Section 65852.2 (as amended through AB 976):

| Standard | State Minimum |
|----------|--------------|
| Max Size — Local cannot restrict below | 850 SF (studio/1BR), 1,000 SF (2+ BR) |
| Height — Detached (standard) | 16 ft |
| Height — Detached (near transit) | 18 ft |
| Height — Attached | 25 ft / 2-story |
| Setbacks — Side & Rear | 4 ft max (local cannot require more) |
| JADU | Must be allowed, up to 500 SF |
| Parking | Exempt within 1/2 mile transit, historic district, in existing structure |
| Owner-Occupancy | Cannot be required (AB 976, permanent) |
| Impact Fees — Under 750 SF | Prohibited |
| Permit Processing | 60 days max |

## Parking Exemption Triggers (State Law)

An ADU is exempt from all parking requirements if ANY of the following apply:
1. Located within 1/2 mile walking distance of a public transit stop
2. Located within an architecturally and historically significant historic district
3. ADU is part of the existing primary residence or an existing accessory structure
4. On-street parking permits are required but not offered to ADU occupant
5. A car-share vehicle is located within one block of the ADU
