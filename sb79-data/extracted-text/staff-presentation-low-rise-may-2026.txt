Key Issues ­ Industrial Land

  At this time, the Department is pending guidance from
        HCD on the applicability of SB 79 on industrial land.

  SB 79 is permitted on sites zoned for "residential, mixed,
        or commercial development", however the City's industrial
        zones permit commercial uses.

  Given health and safety impacts related to allowing
        residential uses on industrial sites, the Department would
        recommend procedures to ensure environmental review
        and mitigation.

  Only industrial sites located within industrial employment

        hubs are eligible for permanent exemption.
