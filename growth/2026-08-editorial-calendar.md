# LandToYield Four-Week Free-First Editorial Calendar

**Run:** August 2026
**Owner:** Trevor Damyan
**Rule:** One verified source asset each week; everything else is a derivative. No invented parcel outcomes, client quotes, or legal conclusions.

## Weekly production rhythm

- **Monday:** Verify official sources and write/update the canonical page.
- **Tuesday:** Publish the canonical page and direct-answer LinkedIn post.
- **Wednesday:** Publish an annotated screenshot/map workflow post.
- **Thursday:** Record a 60–120 second screen demonstration; include the topic in Legislative Edge production.
- **Friday:** Publish the opinion/lesson post and review analytics/questions for FAQ updates.

## Week 1 — Before the offer

**Canonical asset:** `/guides/evaluate-la-development-parcel/`
**Question:** What should a developer verify before making an offer on an LA development parcel?
**Primary CTA:** Analyze a Parcel
**Attorney CTA:** Review the methodology / join the Attorney Council

### LinkedIn 1 — Direct answer

**Hook:** A parcel score is not a diligence plan.
**Body points:** Exact APN; zoning stack; program-specific evidence; existing occupants; physical fit; economics; verification owner.
**Close:** Link to the seven-step guide.

### LinkedIn 2 — Visual workflow

**Asset:** Screenshot showing Discover -> Verify -> Underwrite, with a visible unresolved-data blocker.
**Caption:** “The red flag is sometimes the most valuable output.”
**Do not show:** Unsupported eligible-unit count or fictional address result.

### LinkedIn 3 — Founder opinion

**Hook:** Developers do not need another database. They need a faster first no—or a documented reason to keep spending.
**Question:** Which diligence fact kills the most deals after too much money has already been spent?

### Video

**Title:** “The 90-second LA parcel screen before you write an LOI”
**Shots:** APN entry -> source/verification panel -> assumptions -> blocker/next step.
**Disclaimer:** Preliminary screening; verify current zoning and legal pathway.

## Week 2 — ZIMAS without overconfidence

**Official source:** `planning.lacity.gov/zoning/zoning-search` and `zimas.lacity.org`
**Primary CTA:** Download/use the ZIMAS review checklist.

### LinkedIn 1

**Hook:** The zone string is the start of the analysis, not the answer.
Explain overlays, specific plans, conditions, and source dates.

### LinkedIn 2

**Asset:** Annotated, redacted ZIMAS parcel-profile sections.
Show where to find APN, zoning, General Plan designation, and overlays.

### LinkedIn 3

**Hook:** “What can I build?” is usually five separate questions pretending to be one.
List zoning, program, physical, procedural, and economic questions.

### Video

**Title:** “Three ZIMAS fields developers skip too quickly”
