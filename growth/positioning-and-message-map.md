# LandToYield Positioning and Message Map

## Category

Source-linked preliminary parcel feasibility and development decision support for Los Angeles.

## Core promise

Screen an LA parcel, see plausible development pathways and economics, and identify what must be verified before money or legal conclusions are put at risk.

## Primary audience: developers

- **Trigger:** Address, APN, listing, broker call, off-market lead, or assemblage opportunity.
- **Pain:** Too much time and professional spend before discovering a fatal issue.
- **Outcome:** Reject, request evidence, negotiate diligence, or advance with a documented issue list.
- **CTA:** Analyze a Parcel.
- **Proof:** Source links, dates, visible assumptions, blockers, and sensitivity—not an opaque score.

## Primary audience: land-use attorneys

- **Trigger:** Client intake, acquisition diligence, or legislative-pathway question.
- **Pain:** Incomplete parcel facts, unsupported software claims, and repeated fact gathering.
- **Outcome:** A source-linked preliminary packet and explicit verification list.
- **CTA:** Join the LandToYield Attorney Council.
- **Boundary:** The product accelerates intake; counsel verifies the controlling parcel-specific conclusion.

## Secondary audiences

- **Capital partners:** Structured preliminary collateral and development scenario.
- **Brokers:** Recognition of development questions that may change positioning or buyer pool.
- **Architects/consultants:** Cleaner inputs and a documented assumption set before test-fit work.

## Words to use

- preliminary screen
- parcel identity
- source-linked
- as-of date
- unresolved issue
- development pathway
- editable assumption
- professional verification
- City of Los Angeles coverage

## Words to avoid unless proven for the exact output

- eligible
- approved
- guaranteed
- every LA County parcel
- exact buildable units
- instant zoning determination
- replaces counsel
- investor-ready
- verified (without the evidence and date)

## Three core messages

1. **Faster first decision:** Know whether a parcel deserves deeper work.
2. **Visible uncertainty:** A blocker is surfaced, not averaged into a score.
3. **Better professional handoff:** Developers and counsel work from the same facts, sources, assumptions, and open questions.

## Homepage hierarchy

1. Screen before full diligence.
2. Analyze a Parcel.
3. Developer and attorney pathways.
4. Methodology and trust.
5. How it works.
6. Proof/case examples when available.
7. Pricing after outcome and limitations.

## Standard product-boundary statement

Land to Yield provides preliminary screening and decision support. It does not provide legal advice, a zoning determination, architectural feasibility, survey or title work, appraisal, environmental review, tax advice, or a commitment to lend. Verify current official records and parcel-specific conclusions with qualified professionals.
