## 6. Quality gate

- [ ] Primary sources opened and checked during this production cycle
- [ ] Dates and coverage are explicit
- [ ] No invented client, parcel, quote, metric, or legal conclusion
- [ ] Facts and assumptions are visually distinguishable
- [ ] Claims match the product's fail-closed behavior
- [ ] The same canonical URL is used in every derivative
- [ ] UTM tags identify channel and asset without creating duplicate indexable URLs
- [ ] Trevor approves outbound email or direct outreach before send
