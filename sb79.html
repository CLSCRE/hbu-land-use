The statute

What the statute does.

A summary for orientation. The chaptered text controls.

Gov. Code §§ 65912.155 to 65912.162
What does the statute require?
SB 79 (Wiener, 2025) requires cities to allow housing on eligible sites within one half mile of qualifying transit at statutory minimums for height, density, and floor area. The minimums depend on the transit tier and the distance band. It became operative statewide on July 1, 2026.
Is approval ministerial?
The statute provides a streamlined path for qualifying projects, but qualification is conditional. Affordability, labor, demolition, and site requirements all apply, and a city's implementation can change whether and when the statute reaches a parcel. Whether a specific project qualifies is a parcel and project question for counsel.
Does it require affordable housing?
For most projects, yes. The statute offers tiered affordability options, and projects of 10 units or fewer are exempt from that requirement. State Density Bonus Law can be layered for additional units. Confirm the controlling percentages in the chaptered text before underwriting.
What about parking?
AB 2097 generally bars minimum parking requirements near major transit. This portal's screen carries a 0.5 space per unit assumption in the outer half-mile band from the City's implementation model. The Low-Rise Ordinance's base menu has no parking minimum. Verify for each parcel.
What keeps the statute off a parcel?
Permanent exclusions (Industrial Employment Hubs, and sites without a walking path to the stop under one mile), statutory exemptions (public facilities, parking, and open space zones), protections against demolishing occupied rent-stabilized units, and the temporary exemption pathways in § 65912.161(b), described below.
How did Los Angeles respond?
With two ordinances, both effective June 30, 2026. The Phased Implementation Ordinance (188968) sequences where the statute applies, placing most station-area parcels on temporary hold. The Low-Rise Ordinance (188967) offers a local menu of small multi-unit buildings in residential neighborhoods near transit. Eligibility layers are published in ZIMAS.
