My Portfolio

Every property you analyze is automatically saved here, ranked by listing attractiveness. The key metric is the Opportunity Ratio — what a developer would pay divided by the current market value. Properties at 1.5x or higher are strong listing opportunities where the homeowner stands to gain significantly. Properties below 1.0x are worth more as-is. Use the filter to show only strong opportunities, and click "One-Pager" to generate a polished report for the owner.
